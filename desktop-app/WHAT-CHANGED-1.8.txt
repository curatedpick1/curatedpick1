Curated Studio 1.8

Start typing any product title. The app now immediately suggests a category,
tags and a draft description, even before a photo has been added.

With a photo, Curated Studio asks Gemini for image-aware suggestions and uses
the photo suggestions when they return. If the AI service is unavailable or
returns blank category or tags, the title based suggestions remain filled in.
You can edit every suggested field. Your manual edits are kept.

Install private/Curated-Studio-Setup-1.8.0.exe over the previous version.
Local drafts are kept.
